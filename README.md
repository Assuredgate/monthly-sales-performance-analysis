# Monthly Sales Performance Analysis

## Project Overview
This beginner Data Analytics project examines monthly business performance from January to May using Google Sheets.

## Project Objectives
- Calculate monthly revenue, costs, and profit.
- Calculate profit margins.
- Identify the strongest and weakest months.
- Measure percentage changes in revenue.
- Present findings using a chart and key performance indicators (KPIs).
- Provide evidence-based business recommendations.

## Tools Used
- Google Sheets
- Spreadsheet formulas
- Data analysis and visualisation

## Dataset Summary

| Month | Revenue | Cost | Profit |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| January | ₦200,000 | ₦120,000 | ₦80,000 |
| February | ₦250,000 | ₦150,000 | ₦100,000 |
| March | ₦300,000 | ₦170,000 | ₦130,000 |
| April | ₦220,000 | ₦140,000 | ₦80,000 |
| May | ₦350,000 | ₦200,000 | ₦150,000 |

## Key Findings
- Total revenue: ₦1,320,000.
- Total costs: ₦780,000.
- Total profit: ₦540,000.
- Overall profit margin: 40.91%.
- May had the highest revenue and profit.
- Revenue declined by 26.67% from March to April.
- Revenue increased by 59.09% from April to May.
- April had the lowest monthly profit margin at 36.36%.

## Business Recommendations
1. Investigate the reasons for April's revenue decline.
2. Examine May's sales activity to identify possible contributors to the recovery.
3. Monitor revenue, costs, profit, and profit margins every month.
4. Collect customer and product-level data to support deeper analysis.

## Conclusion
This project demonstrates practical skills in spreadsheet calculations, business performance analysis, visualisation, and communicating findings to support decision-making.
